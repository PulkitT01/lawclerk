## What I learned
- A blobless clone downloads commits first and each file only when it's read. The archive is 42 MB on disk.
- The author date of a commit can differ from the committer date. The 2019 commits were made in 2020.
- `git cat-file blob <commit>:<path>` reads one file as it was at a commit.
- A half-open range (`valid_from <= d < valid_to`) has no gaps and no overlaps between versions.
- Comparing whole sections is safer than comparing chunks: the chunk split changes when a section passes 8000 characters.
- `dataclass` fields with `compare=False` are left out of `==`.
